# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, practical place for people to collaborate. Participants
are expected to treat each other with care, technical honesty, and patience.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be direct about code, claims, and evidence without attacking people.
- Accept constructive feedback and make room for different experience levels.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project and its users' outcomes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption,
and other conduct that makes participation unsafe or hostile are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block participants, or take other
proportionate action when behavior violates this code. Enforcement decisions should focus on the
impact of the behavior and the safety of the community.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to **ehukaimedia@gmail.com**. Include the context, links or screenshots
when available, and whether there is an immediate safety concern.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
